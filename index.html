Built from scratch 05

LogLens — a production log platform

Devs and I were blind to what production was actually doing. So I built and shipped a log-viewing platform to both prod and UAT.

  • Google SSO + roles, per-environment lock, and a per-module dashboard.
  • Date-accurate log fetch, live tail, UTC·IST stamps, and a full file Explorer — "shell access without the shell."
  • Gateway/edge search surfaced the 502/504/timeout traffic the app never logs — used to pinpoint real regressions.
  • Migrated it to systemd — ended the outages where every deploy orphaned the viewer.

Alert → root-cause, in one loop

A monitor email says something changed. LogLens is the "why" — open the flagged server, fetch that exact window, read the crash inline. It closes the loop between alerting and diagnosis.
